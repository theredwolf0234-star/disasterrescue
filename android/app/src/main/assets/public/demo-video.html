Project Overview & Architecture

AISTER (AI Disaster Rescue Coordinator) is an end-to-end mission-critical disaster management system. When natural disasters, flash floods, or seismic tremors collapse infrastructure, AISTER provides resilient emergency triage:

🚨 Instant SOS Beaconing:

High-precision GPS coordinates, casualty count, and offline-first queue synchronization.

🧠 Deterministic AI Risk Engine:

Transparent casualty scoring algorithm (0-100) prioritizing critical threats over minor relief.

🗺️ Tactical Command Dashboard:

Real-time NDRF and SDRF fleet tracking with live status and Dijkstra hazard routing.

⛺ Shelter & Resource Ledger:

Live inventory management for drinking water, ration packets, medical kits, and beds.
