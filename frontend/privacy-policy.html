Privacy Policy for RESCUE AI

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

RESCUE AI ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is committed to protecting your personal information and civil privacy while providing mission-critical emergency triage and disaster coordination capabilities. This Privacy Policy governs our native mobile application and web portals.

⚠️ Emergency Protocol Notice

RESCUE AI operates as an emergency situational awareness and triage platform connecting affected citizens, relief shelters, and first responder squads. It is designed to assist coordination and does not supersede direct state emergency dispatch. In immediate life-threatening scenarios, call national emergency hotline 112 or NDRF hotline 1078 directly.

1. Information We Collect and Why

We only collect data that is strictly essential for dispatching emergency relief, prioritizing high-risk casualties, and ensuring rescuer safety:

📡 Precise Geolocation Data

Permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION.
Purpose: Captures your exact GPS coordinates when you trigger an emergency SOS beacon or navigate to safe relief shelters. We do NOT track your location continuously in the background when the app is inactive.

🎙️ Microphone & Voice Audio

Permissions: RECORD_AUDIO.
Purpose: Powers the hands-free voice SOS engine for users unable to type (e.g. trapped under debris or low mobility). Audio keyword recognition ("SOS", "HELP", "BACHAO") processes on-device. Audio is never recorded for advertising.

📸 Camera & Media Storage

Permissions: CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO.
Purpose: Allows citizens to optionally attach photographic or video evidence of rising floodwaters, structural collapses, or fire hazards to assist command centers in dispatching appropriate equipment (e.g. inflatable boats vs. hydraulic cutters).

👤 User Account & Profile

Data: Full Name, Email Address, and Contact Number.
Purpose: Used by emergency coordinators to confirm survivor identities, prevent duplicate dispatches, and notify family or next of kin regarding rescue status.

2. Information Sharing & Third-Party Services

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or emergency data.

Your data is strictly shared with authorized emergency personnel:

  • Designated Emergency Authorities: Verified incident commanders, NDRF personnel, and field rescue teams logged in to the Tactical Command Console.
  • Relief Shelter Operators: Aggregate headcount data to coordinate food, clean water, and bed capacity.
  • Mapping Providers (Mapbox / OpenStreetMap): Geolocation queries to fetch reverse street addresses and disaster zone overlays.

3. Data Security & Storage

All network transmissions between your mobile device and RESCUE AI servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) and secure WebSockets (WSS). Passwords are encrypted using salted bcrypt hashes, and role-based access control (RBAC) ensures citizens can only inspect their own incident history while authorities access authorized jurisdictional queues.

4. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

Incident reports are maintained during active disaster response operations for operational accountability and post-incident auditing. Users have the complete right under applicable data protection laws to:

  • Request a copy of all personal information and emergency logs associated with their account.
  • Request immediate deletion or anonymization of their account and associated uploaded media once rescue operations conclude.

To request data deletion or privacy inquiries, contact our Data Governance Officer at: privacy@rescueai.emergency or submit through the portal contact form.

5. Children's Privacy

RESCUE AI does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13 for non-emergency purposes. In disaster scenarios where minors are reported as affected victims, their information is utilized solely to facilitate location by family members and emergency rescue squads.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect modifications in emergency management regulations or Google Play Developer Policy requirements. Any material changes will be announced directly within the mobile application and updated with a revised effective date on this page.
