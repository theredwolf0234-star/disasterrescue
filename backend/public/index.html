Disaster Safety & Preparedness Guide

Standard operating procedures, offline emergency protocols, and first-aid instructions.

🌊 Flood Safety Checklist
  • Move immediately to higher ground or upper building levels.
  • Do not walk, swim, or drive through moving flood waters. Turn around, don't drown.
  • Disconnect electrical appliances and main breaker if safe to do so.
  • Drink boiled or bottled water only; avoid tap or flood runoff.
  • Keep your mobile phone charged and store emergency phone numbers offline.
🔥 Fire & Smoke Evacuation
  • Stay low to the floor where air is cleaner; crawl under smoke.
  • Feel doors with the back of your hand before opening. If hot, do not open.
  • Never use elevators during a fire evacuation; use emergency fire stairs.
  • Stop, Drop, and Roll if clothing catches fire.
  • Call 112 or 101 once safely outside.
⛰️ Earthquake Protocol
  • DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON: Drop under sturdy furniture and hold on.
  • Stay away from glass windows, exterior walls, and tall loose furniture.
  • If outdoors, move to an open area away from power lines and trees.
  • Expect aftershocks and prepare for structural inspections before re-entering.
📶 Offline & Poor Connectivity Mode
  • When cellular or internet connection is lost, SOS beacons are cached locally on your device.
  • Keep the browser tab open; the application will automatically transmit the beacon the moment connectivity returns.
  • Use SMS or voice telephony to 112 as an immediate fallback if cellular voice is functional.
  • Conserve device battery by enabling extreme power saving mode.

AI Engine Model Transparency

The risk engine combines multi-criteria meteorology telemetry (rainfall, elevation, wind speeds) with victim headcount weights and heuristic computer vision. AI vision and risk assessments provide advisory assistance and are explicitly not 100% accurate.
